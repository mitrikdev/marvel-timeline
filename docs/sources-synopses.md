# Film synopsis sources

Reviewed September 12, 2026. The 77 entries in src/data/synopses.ts are original editorial summaries, each 25–45 words, based on the published sources below. They describe the premise and early conflict rather than providing a full plot recap. A sequel's starting situation can reveal events from earlier films.

The application bundles these summaries locally. Opening a film does not call an external service. Every record has its own source link; the full key set is checked against the catalog in tests/synopses.test.ts.

## Research approach

Official film, studio and distributor pages were preferred and their published premises were read through web research. Indexed official-page text was used where a direct visit returned an access error. Wording was rewritten rather than copied. Source links document the premise, not a claim that each official page's credits or dates are error-free.

For the 2025–2026 releases, Captain America: Brave New World, Thunderbolts*, The Fantastic Four: First Steps and Spider-Man: Brand New Day use their official Disney or Sony premises. Brand New Day's entry follows the published Sony setup and adds no speculative villain identities or resolution.

Some older pages lack useful plot text. The 20th Century Studios X-Men page describes its sequel, and its The Last Stand text contains contradictory details, so those blurbs were not used. The Deadpool pages contain joke descriptions; their factual Disney+ descriptions were used instead. The Blade and Punisher films use Wikipedia because the Warner Bros. and Lionsgate pages did not expose readable synopses. Iron Man uses the American Film Institute; Iron Man 2 and Venom: Let There Be Carnage use Wikipedia for clearer plot coverage.
