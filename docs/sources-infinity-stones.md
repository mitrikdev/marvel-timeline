# Infinity Stone journeys

Reviewed 12 September 2026. The module contains six selectable artifacts and 33 film/stone stops across 13 existing films. Dates remain the catalog's US theatrical release dates; a summary's 1970, 1995, 2012, 2013, or 2014 refers to story chronology, not sorting.

## Curation rules

- One row per film/stone. The strongest qualifying appearance determines its role.
- `supporting`: a physical stone or its established container materially participates in the main story.
- `cameo`: a brief container appearance or explicitly described vision/presentation. A depicted stone does not imply physical custody.
- `post-credit`: the film's qualifying appearance occurs only during credits. A main-story appearance followed by a credits appearance remains `supporting`.
- These are artifacts, not performers. They do not receive actor credits.
- Aliases help search for containers; the Tesseract, Orb, scepter, and Eye are not separate stones.
- Journey summaries are original paraphrases and contain plot spoilers.

## Visions and presentations

The Guardians scene receives six entries: Power is physically present in the Orb; the other five are presentation cameos, including Soul. [James Gunn's firsthand interview](https://comicbook.com/marvel/news/james-gunn-infinity-saga-backstory-writing-first-guardians-of-the-galaxy-movie-exclusive/) discusses creating the scene, and its accompanying report describes the designs of all six. Treating each design as a journey cameo is an editorial interpretation of the presentation. It does not move every stone to Knowhere or replace their later physical introductions.

Age of Ultron receives four entries: physical Mind, plus Space, Reality, and Power as visions. [D23](https://d23.com/celebrate-thors-10-year-mcu-anniversary-with-his-top-10-moments/) confirms Thor's revelation but does not enumerate every image. The exact three non-Mind identifications are cross-checked against this [scene discussion](https://scifi.stackexchange.com/questions/258338/why-doesnt-thor-see-the-time-stone-or-the-soul-stone-in-his-dream); this is secondary scene evidence, not a studio inventory. Time and Soul are not added to that vision.

## Endgame variants

The original stones have already been destroyed when the Avengers confront Thanos. That destruction is reported, not shown as a separate six-stone scene. [Marvel's War Machine history](https://www.marvel.com/characters/war-machine-james-rhodes/on-screen/) confirms this distinction.

The six rows instead follow borrowed past versions: Space from 1970 after the failed 2012 attempt, Mind and Time from 2012, Reality from 2013, and Power and Soul from 2014. Hulk uses the set to restore the vanished; Tony later uses it against Thanos's army. Steve departs to return the borrowed stones. These are six artifact identities across variants, not claims that one uninterrupted physical set survives the destruction.

## Excluded or deferred cases

- Odin's decorative gauntlet in Thor/Ragnarok does not establish six appearances. [Kevin Feige's firsthand interview](https://www.slashfilm.com/554232/thor-ragnarok-spoiler-questions/) confirms it is fake. Age of Ultron's credits gauntlet is empty.
- The Dark World's dialogue about the Tesseract remaining in Asgard is not a Space appearance. Neither are discussions of stones in Guardians Vol. 2.
- No Way Home's Eye is not evidence for a surviving Time Stone: [D23](https://d23.com/doctor-stranges-maddening-journey-through-the-mcu/) explicitly recounts Strange saying he no longer has it.
- Background sketches, logos, memorial photographs, and ordinary colored jewels are outside this curated route. A filmed instructional presentation or supernatural vision is labeled separately.
- Multiverse of Madness has an alternate Thanos/gauntlet flashback. The exact stones remain deferred rather than marked absent: [this report and film still](https://thedirect.com/article/doctor-strange-2-avengers-endgame-alternate-ending) explicitly find their number difficult to establish. It does not securely verify a five-stone list.
- Deadpool & Wolverine's red/green ring gems are also deferred. Disney's [official screenplay](https://assets.debut.disney.com/documents/Deadpool_Book_Single_Pages.pdf), printed page 105 (PDF page 115), identifies Doctor Strange's ring but does not name Infinity Stones. Color alone is insufficient to add Reality/Time credits.
- This is a documented set of story appearances, not a frame-by-frame inventory of every film in the 77-title catalog.
