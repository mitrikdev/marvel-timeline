# MCU character identities

Reviewed 12 September 2026. Scope: existing MCU-derived catalog character IDs, with the shared Adrian Toomes and Thaddeus Ross entries assigned to this module. No appearances, casting, or character IDs were added.

The catalog keeps identities shared by different people as separate records: Steve Rogers and Sam Wilson are both Captain America; Natasha Romanoff and Yelena Belova both have the Black Widow designation. Display names describe identities established in screen stories or official screen-specific publicity, rather than exclusive ownership of a mantle. Historical mantles and names remain searchable aliases.

Common screen identities are also retained from the films represented by the catalog, including Red Skull / Johann Schmidt, Abomination / Emil Blonsky, Iron Monger / Obadiah Stane, Star-Lord / Peter Quill, Mysterio / Quentin Beck, and the original Ant-Man and Wasp. These entries are editorial identity normalization, not additional appearance claims. Peggy Carter's Captain Carter identity refers to her alternate-universe film appearance; historical Agent Carter remains an alias.

Marvel’s [Silver Centurion retrospective](https://www.marvel.com/articles/comics/iron-man-of-the-80s-the-silver-centurion?linkId=67483507) also explicitly connects Obadiah Stane’s Iron Monger armor to the first Iron Man film. Its discussion of unrelated comic events is not used for the catalog.

## Exclusions and navigation choices

- Monica Rambeau is not labeled Photon, Spectrum, Pulsar, or Captain Marvel. No unsupported comic identity is promoted to a film identity.
- Maria Rambeau's distinct Captain Marvel and Binary variants remain under her canonical name in this module rather than merging their variant-specific identities indiscriminately.
- Cassie Lang is not labeled Stature or Stinger; Dane Whitman is not labeled Black Knight; Melina Vostokoff is not labeled Iron Maiden; Ruth Bat-Seraph is not labeled Sabra.
- Mac Gargan remains searchable by his existing name. [Official film-specific Funko merchandise](https://funko.com/pop-scorpion-brand-new-day/92040.html) establishes a Scorpion figure, and [Sony's film page](https://www.sonypictures.com/movies/spidermanbrandnewday) lists Michael Mando, but the checked studio text did not directly pair Mac Gargan with that codename. No comic-only MacDonald expansion was added.
- No comic-only civilian identities are invented for Valkyrie, Kang, the Grandmaster, the High Evolutionary, Galactus, or the Ancient One.
- Wenwu and Trevor Slattery are not relabeled Mandarin. That term involves a disavowed label and an impersonation in the films.
- Yellowjacket remains Darren Cross's primary navigation label; M.O.D.O.K. and MODOK also find the same existing character.
- The villain list records substantial screen antagonists and rivals, including antagonists already known under their sole names. It does not assert that they are villains in every appearance.
- Loki, Nebula, Wanda, Bucky, Ghost, Taskmaster, Yelena, Red Guardian, John Walker, Sentry, Ikaris, and Sprite retain their established team navigation homes. Their hostile or coerced appearances do not automatically override those assignments.
- Shared legacy identities such as Spider-Man, Hulk, Wolverine, and the Fantastic Four are supplied by the legacy identity module; this module does not overwrite them.
