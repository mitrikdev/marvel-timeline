# MCU data curation

Checked September 12, 2026. The 38 records in `src/data/mcu.ts` follow the exact Marvel Studios list in the build spec. Every `sourceUrls` entry was located through web search or an opened official page during this build; URLs were not generated from film titles.

## Scope and editorial decisions

- Dates mean original US theatrical release, including July 2, 2019 for _Far From Home_. They do not mean premiere, international opening, streaming release, or re-release.
- Public studio pages document release and principal cast information. Appearances additionally curate familiar supporting roles, crossover characters, and credits scenes; these pages are not exhaustive end-credit transcripts. Lead/supporting/cameo is an editorial classification for exploring the map, not a contractual credit or exact screen-time measure.
- The `post-credit` role includes mid-credit and end-credit scenes when that is the character's only appearance. A character who also appears in the story receives their story role. Promotional trailers shown after a film are excluded.
- Archive montages, photographs, memorial images, and non-speaking background doubles are not treated as new appearances. Recasts remain the same character with distinct actor records.
- Production labels are normalized to major studio families; distributors alone do not become production studios. For example, Universal's distribution of _The Incredible Hulk_ does not give it the Universal production label. Sony's Spider-Man films carry Columbia, Sony, and Marvel Studios labels.
- _The Fantastic Four: First Steps_ belongs to the Marvel Studios / MCU franchise family but has a separate lane for its retro-futuristic Earth.
- Earth-838 in _Multiverse of Madness_ and the alternate Earth in _The Marvels_ are grouped with the MCU multiverse. Familiar actors alone do not establish a Fox or older Fantastic Four continuity crossover.
- _Deadpool & Wolverine_ is placed in the MCU lane as requested. Its legacy connections use broad origin-family lanes. The lead Wolverine is a variant, and Tatum's Gambit connects to the Fox-era development history rather than appearing in an earlier released Gambit film.
- In _Thor: The Dark World_, Chris Evans plays Loki impersonating Steve Rogers. In _Far From Home_, Samuel L. Jackson and Cobie Smulders largely play Talos and Soren impersonating Fury and Hill; the real Fury has a credits-scene appearance. These are preserved as separate character/actor mappings.

## Important source distinctions

Disney movie pages sometimes list international dates. _Black Panther_ is therefore dated February 16, 2018 using [Disney's explicitly US announcement](https://d23.com/black-panthers-new-trailer-and-cocos-big-win-plus-more-in-news-briefs/), and _Captain Marvel_ is dated March 8, 2019 using [D23's release coverage](https://d23.com/shes-the-captain/).

Marvel's [2008 annual filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/933730/000136231009002976/c81637e10vk.htm) verifies the actual May 2 and June 13, 2008 releases of _Iron Man_ and _The Incredible Hulk_. Its proposed dates for later films were not used. Paramount's [Iron Man 2 release announcement](https://ir.paramount.com/news-releases/news-release-details/marvel-entertainment-and-paramount-pictures-iron-man-2-be) and [Captain America premiere announcement](https://ir.paramount.com/static-files/ccef1606-3ee5-424a-9738-bef8eaaee177) cover the early films whose current Marvel pages could not be opened by the research tool.

_Spider-Man: Brand New Day_ uses [Sony's official movie page](https://www.sonypictures.com/movies/spidermanbrandnewday), [Sony's official June 2026 trailer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Rv1dodCKgvE), and [Sony's dated US/Japan release announcement](https://www.sonypictures.jp/corp/press/2026-03-18-0). The record deliberately includes only established returning character identities from verified studio materials. New-character identities and post-credit additions need a verified credits source before being added; social speculation is not data.

The film detail links expose the primary sources for each record. Curation is intentionally useful rather than encyclopedic, and may be expanded with verified appearances.
